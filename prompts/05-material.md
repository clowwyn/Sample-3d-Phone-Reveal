## 1. SUBJECT

An extreme close-up (2:1 macro) of the NOVA Elite frame-to-back-glass transition. The phone has been physically turned from back-facing to side-facing — the camera module is no longer in view. The shot shows the precision engineering of the material transition: the titanium frame meeting the black glass back panel.

**NOVA Elite Frame-to-Glass Transition Canonical Specs:**

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| Frame Material | Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) — aerospace-grade |
| Frame Finish | Brushed directional finish with 120-grit texture |
| Brushing Direction | Vertical (top-to-bottom) — MUST be visible |
| Frame Color | Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A) |
| Edge Treatment | Micro-beveled at 0.2mm width, 15-degree catch angle |
| Frame-to-Glass Joint | 0.3mm black rubber gasket — visible as hairline shadow line |
| Back Material | Optically polished black glass, Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2 |
| Back Texture | Micro-prismatic texture — visible at macro scale |
| Back Color | Midnight Obsidian (#0D0D0D) with 5% light transmission |
| Oleophobic Coating | Visible as slight sheen reduction on glass surface |
| Anti-Reflective Coating | Visible as subtle color shift (slight blue-green) at glass edges |

## 4. MOVEMENT

**Macro slider, longitudinal.** The camera moves toward the subject along its optical axis — the lens focuses as it moves. This feels like a lens focusing, intimate and deliberate.

**Movement speed:** 0.04 m/sec (12 seconds for full travel). Over 3 seconds, the camera moves approximately 0.12 meters toward the subject.

**Camera path:** Straight toward the subject, along the optical axis. The movement is longitudinal — in and out — not side-to-side. The camera is "breathing" toward the material.

**No handheld movement. No shake. No vibration.** The macro slider is precision-engineered.

## 5. LIGHTING

**Two-light setup with cross-polarization:**

**Key Light:**
| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Instrument | ARRI SkyPanel S60-C |
| Position | 15 degrees side angle |
| Color Temperature | 5600K |
| Modification | Linear polarizer on light source |
| Purpose | Cross-polarization eliminates unwanted reflections on glass |

**Rim Light:**
| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Instrument | Dedolight DLH400D |
| Position | 90 degrees side |
| Color Temperature | 5600K |
| Modification | Tight beam, wide barndoors |
| Purpose | Defines the frame edge against the back glass |

**Cross-Polarization:** Yes — linear polarizer on light source, analyzer on camera, 90 degrees crossed. This eliminates specular glare from the titanium frame and glass surface, allowing the underlying texture to be seen clearly.

**Ambient:** None — maximum contrast control is required at this macro scale.

**Atmosphere:** None (0%) — any particle would be distracting.

**Result:** Frame surface texture (120-grit vertical brushing) is clearly visible. The micro-bevel edge is defined. The glass texture is visible without overwhelming reflections. The transition line (0.3mm gasket) is visible as a hairline shadow between frame and glass.

## 6. MATERIALS

**Frame — Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) at 2:1 magnification:**

- **Brushed vertical texture:** At 2:1 magnification, individual brush strokes from the 120-grit texture are visible. The vertical direction of the brushing (top-to-bottom) is clearly visible and must be respected. This is a distinctive identifier of the NOVA Elite.
- **Frame color:** Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A) — warm gray metallic. The cross-polarization reveals the true color without specular distortion.
- **Micro-bevel:** The 0.2mm bevel at the frame edge catches the side light at 15-degree angles. This bevel is where the frame meets the air, and it creates a distinct highlight.
- **Fingerprint resistance:** The micro-texture provides fingerprint resistance — this texture is visible at this magnification.

**Back Glass — Midnight Obsidian (#0D0D0D):**

- **Micro-prismatic texture:** At 2:1 magnification, the subtle diffusion texture of the glass is visible. This texture reduces fingerprint visibility by 85%.
- **Light transmission:** The 5% light transmission means the glass appears as deep charcoal, not pure black.
- **Oleophobic coating:** Visible as a slight reduction in sheen compared to untreated glass.
- **Anti-reflective coating:** Visible as a subtle color shift (slight blue-green) at the glass edges where light is glancing.

**Frame-to-Glass Transition:**

- **Precision gap:** The 0.3mm gap is filled with black rubber gasket.
- **Hairline shadow:** The gasket creates a thin dark line between the titanium frame and the glass back.
- **Chamfered edge:** Both the frame and glass have 0.2mm chamfered edges at the joint.
- **Flush appearance:** Despite the gasket, the joint appears flush — the precision of the engineering is in the tight tolerance.

## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Offset diagonal. The frame-to-glass transition line runs diagonally across the frame — from upper-left to lower-right or vice versa. This diagonal composition leads the eye through the material transition.

**Grid:** Golden ratio. The composition follows the golden ratio — the frame and glass are positioned according to the golden ratio proportions within the frame.

**Angle:** Slight side angle (15 degrees). The camera is positioned at a 15-degree angle to the frame edge, showing both the frame surface texture and the glass surface texture simultaneously.

**Subject:** The frame-to-glass transition line is the primary subject. The composition should show both materials clearly, with the transition line in clear focus.

**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (CinemaScope). The wide frame allows the diagonal composition to breathe.
