## PREAMBLE

This document defines the complete shot sequence for the NOVA Elite flagship smartphone reveal commercial. Every shot is specified with precise technical parameters drawn from the canonical product specification and cinematography documents. The storyboard serves as a production-ready blueprint that any director or generator can execute without ambiguity.

**Narrative Arc:** MYSTERY → ANTICIPATION → DISCOVERY → DETAIL → CRAFTSMANSHIP → TECHNOLOGY → HERO
**Shot Count:** 9 shots (fixed)
**Product:** NOVA Elite (canonical — see product-spec.md)
**Phone Consistency Rule:** The phone is ONE physical object. No redesigns across any shot.
