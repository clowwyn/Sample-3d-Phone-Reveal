# SHOT 01 — INTRODUCTION / DARKNESS
## Production Prompt

---

**Shot Number:** 01
**Title:** DARKNESS
**Narrative Stage:** MYSTERY
**Duration:** 4 seconds
**Emotional Target:** Curiosity, quiet intrigue

---

## 1. SUBJECT

The NOVA Elite flagship smartphone is positioned lying flat on a black studio surface, back panel facing upward. Only a single catchlight touches the top-right edge of the titanium frame. The phone is nearly invisible — 95% of the frame is pure shadow.

**NOVA Elite Canonical Specs (referenced exactly):**
- Dimensions: 165.1mm tall x 77.6mm wide x 8.26mm deep
- Frame material: Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V), brushed vertical finish
- Frame color: Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A)
- Back material: Optically polished black glass, Midnight Obsidian (#0D0D0D)
- Back finish: Matte etched, micro-prismatic texture
- Camera module: Rounded rectangle, 52mm x 38mm, top-left position
- Lens arrangement: 3 lenses in vertical column (top: Periscope f/3.0, middle: Main f/1.78, bottom: Ultra-wide f/2.2)
- No logo visible at this distance
- No UI elements, no text, no branding

---

## 2. ENVIRONMENT

**Studio black void** — absolute darkness, no environmental detail. Background: pure black (#0A0A0A). The phone sits on a surface that is invisible. There is no gradient, no texture, no horizon line. The world consists only of the phone, the light, and the void.

---

## 3. CAMERA

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Focal Length | 35mm prime |
| f-Stop | f/1.4 (wide open) |
| Sensor | VistaVision-equivalent (53.7 x 40.2mm) |
| Lens Character | Cooke S7/i or equivalent — organic warmth, soft edge falloff |
| Aspect Ratio | 2.39:1 (CinemaScope) |
| Camera Type | Locked tripod |
| Angle | Eye-level, slightly low |
| Framing | Edge-weighted — catchlight at extreme frame edge, phone at edge of darkness |
| Grid | Loose rule of thirds |

The camera is absolutely still. There is no movement except for a micro-drift of 0.02 degrees per second — barely perceptible, just enough to suggest the phone exists in real space.

---

## 4. MOVEMENT

**Locked tripod with micro-drift.** The camera does not move. The phone does not move. The only movement is a subtle micro-drift of 0.02 degrees per second — a barely perceptible rotation that suggests the camera is alive but resting. This creates tension: the viewer senses something is about to happen.

**Movement speed:** 0.02 deg/sec (micro-drift). Locked for 4 seconds total.

---

## 5. LIGHTING

**Primary instrument:** Dedolight DLH400D — single source, positioned above-right, at the extreme edge of the frame. This is an edge light, not a key light.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Instrument | Dedolight DLH400D |
| Position | Above-right, edge of frame, raking across phone edge |
| Color Temperature | 3200K (tungsten warm) |
| Output | Minimum (10%) |
| Modification | Tight barndoors to keep light contained |
| Ambient | Cineo Lighting Air at 2% — no direction, just enough to suggest volume |

**Result:** A single catchlight traces the titanium frame profile at the top-right edge. The brushed vertical texture catches the light at glancing angles. The phone is barely present — only the 15-degree micro-beveled frame edge catches enough light to exist.

**No fill. No bounce. No secondary sources.** This is a single-point-of-light moment.

---

## 6. MATERIALS

**Frame — Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V):**
- Brushed directional finish with 120-grit vertical texture
- The light catches individual brush strokes at grazing angles
- Micro-beveled edge at 0.2mm width, 15-degree catch angle — this catches the Dedolight
- Frame color: Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A) — warm gray metallic

**Back Glass — Midnight Obsidian:**
- Optically polished black glass, #0D0D0D
- Matte etched finish reduces fingerprint visibility
- Micro-prismatic texture visible only at extreme angles
- 5% light transmission — the glass appears as pure void until illuminated

**Camera Module:**
- Applied Titanium bezel matching frame
- Brushed vertical texture matching frame
- 0.3mm chamfered step from back glass

---

## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Edge-weighted. The phone is positioned so that only the top-right corner — where the frame edge catches the Dedolight — is at the extreme edge of the frame. The catchlight traces the frame profile, revealing the vertical brushed texture.

**Grid:** Loose rule of thirds. The phone occupies approximately the lower-right third. Upper-left is pure void.

**Angle:** Eye-level, slightly low. The camera is positioned so that the viewer is looking at the phone from slightly below eye level — discovering it, not examining it.

**Leading room:** The phone faces upper-right in frame. There is breathing room in the direction the catchlight leads.

---

## 8. CONTINUITY CONSTRAINTS

This is the opening shot. It establishes the visual language:
- Studio black void environment (continues through shots 01-06)
- Single-edge lighting treatment
- Near-total darkness (95% shadow)
- The phone lies flat, back facing up — this orientation continues through shots 01-04
- Color temperature: 3200K (tungsten warm) — the only warm shot in the sequence
- Light haze at 2% density — catches the single light beam

**Must match subsequent shots:**
- Phone dimensions: 165.1mm x 77.6mm (proportional)
- Frame color: #8E8D8A Applied Titanium
- Back glass: #0D0D0D Midnight Obsidian
- Camera module visible as a dark mass (not yet detailed)

---

## 9. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

**DO NOT include:**
- No clear phone silhouette — the phone must be nearly invisible
- No camera module detail visible
- No logo or wordmark
- No UI elements or text
- No front face or display
- No buttons or ports visible
- No frame grain detail visible at this distance
- No depth or dimension clearly readable
- No additional objects in frame
- No colored gels or theatrical lighting
- No haze beyond 2% density
- No random particles or dust motes
- No anamorphic distortion or barrel distortion
- No handheld movement or shake
- No whip pans or Dutch angles
- No harsh specular highlights beyond the single catchlight
- No environment or set dressing of any kind

---

## 10. DURATION

**4 seconds.** The camera is locked. The light is fixed. The viewer waits. The stillness is intentional — it creates tension and anticipation. The shot ends with a hard cut to Shot 02 (SILHOUETTE) as the catchlight reaches its peak intensity.

---

## 11. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

**What the viewer should feel:**
- A sense of profound quiet and mystery
- Curiosity about what exists in the darkness
- The anticipation that something significant is about to be revealed
- The sense of discovering a rare artifact in a museum setting

**What the shot must accomplish:**
- A single catchlight on the phone's titanium frame edge is visible
- The catchlight traces the brushed vertical texture of the frame
- The phone appears nearly invisible — 95% shadow
- The viewer cannot yet identify the phone as a smartphone
- The shot reads as "something is there" — not "here is a phone"
