## 1. SUBJECT

The NOVA Elite displayed at a slight 3/4 angle, showing both the display and the frame, with one defining feature highlighted through dramatic lighting. The preferred feature is the camera system — one lens is dramatically lit, showing the multi-layer AR coating's color-shifted reflections and the precision of the lens array. The phone exists in a real-feeling environment for the first time — a dark architectural space with concrete walls.

**NOVA Elite Feature Highlight Canonical Specs:**

**Primary Feature: Camera System (Lens Detail)**
- One lens (preferably the Main, middle lens, 11.2mm diameter) is dramatically lit
- The multi-layer AR coating on the synthetic sapphire cover creates subtle color-shifted reflections (green/magenta)
- The lens shows refraction of the spotlight — light bends through the sapphire element
- The Periscope prism mechanism (90-degree optical path fold) may be visible in the top lens

**Phone Position and Orientation:**
- Phone at slight 3/4 angle
- Both display and frame visible
- Frame brushed vertical texture visible on all visible edges
- Camera module visible (0.3mm titanium gutter shadow line visible)

**Display Element (Brief Illumination):**
- A brief camera interface or computational photography demo
- Abstract — not a full UI
- The display illumination briefly lights the phone's frame

## 2. ENVIRONMENT

**Dark architectural space (concrete, minimal).** This is the first time the sequence leaves the studio black void — the world returns. Concrete walls at approximately 3m distance from the phone, dark, minimal. The space suggests an industrial or museum setting without being explicit. The space is real and felt, but not detailed.

**Atmosphere:** Light haze at 12% density (the highest haze level in the sequence). The spotlight cutting through the haze is part of the drama — visible light beams add depth to the dark space.

---

## 4. MOVEMENT

**Gimbal orbital.** The camera physically circles the phone on a 1.2m radius. The phone is the center of the orbit. This movement allows the camera to show the feature from multiple angles while maintaining the phone as the focal point.

**Movement speed:** 0.5 m/sec arc speed (8-10 seconds per 180-degree arc). Over 3 seconds, the camera orbits approximately 54-67 degrees of the full 360-degree circle.

**Camera path:** Circular arc around the phone, maintaining 1.2m radius constant. The camera does not push in or pull out — only orbits. The movement is smooth, gimbal-stabilized, and respectful of the product.

**No handheld movement. No shake. No vibration.** The gimbal provides perfect stabilization.

---

## 5. LIGHTING

**Single dramatic spotlight with hard-edged shadow:**

**Primary Instrument:**
| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Instrument | ARRI L7-C Fresnel |
| Position | Top-down 90 degrees (directly above the phone) |
| Color Temperature | 5600K (native) |
| Modification | Full CTB (Color Temperature Blue, 1/2), 8-degree spot |
| Output | Full (100%) — creates hard-edged shadow |
| Purpose | Creates one hard-edged shadow on the phone face, defining the feature spatially |

**Result:** The spotlight comes from directly above, creating a single hard-edged shadow that defines the feature spatially. One lens is dramatically lit, showing the AR coating reflections. The light cuts through the 12% haze, creating a visible beam.

**No fill light. No bounce. No secondary sources.** The single source creates dramatic contrast — the feature is revealed through light, not soft fill.

**Display Self-Illumination:**
- Brief illumination as the display activates
- Simulated UI element or computational photography demo
- The display briefly lights the phone's frame from the front

## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Lower-third. The phone is positioned in the lower third of the frame, with the upper two-thirds as breathing room. The concrete environment is visible in the background.

**Grid:** Rule of thirds. The phone's center is positioned at one of the rule of thirds intersections. The highlighted lens is positioned at the primary intersection point.

**Angle:** Low to eye-level. The camera is positioned slightly below eye level, looking up at the phone. This gives the phone a sense of monumentality and importance.

**Subject:** The phone with feature highlighted. The camera lens is the primary subject, but the phone exists in context — the concrete environment is visible in the background.

**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (CinemaScope). The wide frame allows the phone and environment to be shown together.

**Orbit composition:** As the camera orbits, the relationship between the phone and the background changes. The phone remains the center of attention, but the environment provides context and depth.
