# SHOT 02 — SILHOUETTE
## Production Prompt

---

**Shot Number:** 02
**Title:** SILHOUETTE
**Narrative Stage:** ANTICIPATION
**Duration:** 3.5 seconds
**Emotional Target:** Growing expectation, focused attention

---

## 1. SUBJECT

The NOVA Elite is now visible as a complete silhouette against a pure black background. The phone lies flat, back panel facing camera. The silhouette reveals the phone's complete form: a 165.1mm x 77.6mm rectangle with 9.0mm corner radius. The three-lens camera module appears as a darker mass in the top-left quadrant. The phone's depth (8.26mm body + 3.14mm camera protrusion) creates a slight shadow depth. The viewer can now confirm "this is a smartphone" but cannot yet see materials, color, or detail.

**NOVA Elite Canonical Specs (visible in silhouette):**
- Dimensions: 165.1mm tall x 77.6mm wide
- Corner radius: 9.0mm (all four corners, uniform)
- Body depth: 8.26mm
- Camera module: Top-left quadrant, 52mm x 38mm, rounded rectangle with 12mm corner radius
- Camera module protrusion: 3.14mm beyond back surface
- Three lenses in vertical column arrangement (Periscope top, Main middle, Ultra-wide bottom)
- No materials or color visible — pure silhouette

---

## 2. ENVIRONMENT

**Studio black void** — absolute black (#0A0A0A). No gradient, no texture, no horizon. The phone floats in pure darkness, defined only by the backlight's rim glow. The background must read as physically real darkness — not as a black card or a digital black.

---

## 3. CAMERA

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Focal Length | 50mm prime |
| f-Stop | f/2.0 |
| Sensor | VistaVision-equivalent (53.7 x 40.2mm) |
| Lens Character | Cooke S7/i or equivalent — organic warmth, soft edge falloff |
| Aspect Ratio | 2.39:1 (CinemaScope) |
| Camera Type | Slow dolly push |
| Angle | Eye-level, slightly low |
| Framing | Center-low — phone positioned in lower-center of frame |
| Grid | Center axis — perfect vertical symmetry |

The 50mm focal length provides natural human-eye compression. The phone reads as a single object occupying the lower-center of the frame. There is perfect vertical symmetry — the camera's center axis aligns with the phone's vertical reveal axis.

---

## 4. MOVEMENT

**Slow dolly push.** The camera physically moves forward toward the phone at 0.3 m/sec — one meter takes 3.3 seconds. Over 3.5 seconds, the camera moves approximately 1.05 meters. This is a slow, deliberate approach that builds anticipation.

**Movement speed:** 0.3 m/sec (3.3 seconds per 1m of travel).

**Camera path:** Straight forward, on a center axis aligned with the phone's vertical center. The camera does not arc or curve. It pushes directly toward the subject.

**No handheld movement. No shake. No vibration.** The dolly moves on precision tracks.

---

## 5. LIGHTING

**Primary instrument:** ARRI Orbital SkyPanel S60-C, positioned high and behind the phone, providing pure backlight.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Instrument | ARRI Orbital SkyPanel S60-C |
| Position | High, behind phone (behind subject, facing camera) |
| Color Temperature | 5600K (daylight) |
| Output | Full (100%) |
| Modification | Full blue (CTB 1/2), no diffusion |
| Fill | None — phone is fully backlit, no front illumination |
| Atmosphere | None (0%) — clean silhouette, no haze |

**Result:** Pure silhouette with a defined rim edge. The backlight wraps around the phone's contours, creating a clean glow that traces the frame profile. The three-lens camera module appears as a darker mass within the silhouette because the lenses do not transmit light — they appear as voids against the backlit frame.

**No front key light. No fill. No bounce.** The phone is backlit only.

**Continuity from Shot 01:** The single catchlight from Shot 01 has expanded into the full backlight. The light source "prefigures" the match cut to Shot 03's key light — one light source, two moments.

---

## 6. MATERIALS

**All materials appear as pure shadow in this shot.** The lighting does not allow the viewer to see the phone's materials. The frame is silhouetted. The back glass is silhouetted. The camera module bezel is silhouetted. Only the SHAPES are visible.

**However, the materials should be physically correct in the render:**
- Frame: Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) with Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A) finish
- Back: Optically polished black glass, Midnight Obsidian (#0D0D0D)
- Camera module: Applied Titanium (#8E8D8A) bezel
- Lens covers: Synthetic sapphire

**Light behavior:** The backlight does not penetrate the materials (no subsurface scattering visible). The phone reads as a true silhouette — opaque, unilluminated from the front. The rim glow from the backlight is the only illumination on the phone's edges.

---

## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Center-low. The phone is positioned in the lower-center of the frame. The upper half of the frame is pure void, allowing the phone to "rise" from the darkness as the camera pushes in.

**Grid:** Center axis. The phone's vertical center aligns perfectly with the frame's vertical center. This creates absolute symmetry — the phone is the only subject, and it is presented with reverence.

**Angle:** Eye-level, slightly low. The viewer is looking at the phone from slightly below eye level — discovering it, not examining it. The slight low angle gives the phone a sense of monumentality.

**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (CinemaScope). The wide frame emphasizes the phone's horizontal proportions and creates breathing room above and below.

**Reveal axis:** The camera's center axis aligns with the phone's vertical reveal axis — the imaginary line down the phone's center. This axis will be consistent across all subsequent shots.

---

- **Color temperature shift:** 3200K (warm) in Shot 01 → 5600K (neutral daylight) in Shot 02

**Must match subsequent shots (03-09):**
- Phone dimensions: 165.1mm x 77.6mm
- Camera module: Top-left, 52mm x 38mm, rounded rectangle
- Three lenses in vertical column
- 9.0mm corner radius
- Camera module protrusion: 3.14mm

**Match cut to Shot 03:** The silhouette backlight in Shot 02 matches the key light in Shot 03. As the camera completes the dolly, the cut happens — the backlight becomes the key light from the opposite angle.

**3.5 seconds.** The camera dollies forward continuously. At 0.3 m/sec, the camera moves approximately 1.05 meters in 3.5 seconds. The dolly is continuous — no acceleration, no deceleration, no pauses. The shot ends with a match cut to Shot 03 as the camera reaches near-center position.

**What the shot must accomplish:**
