| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|

- Sensor: 1/3.1"

**Lens 2 — Main (Middle):**

- OIS: Yes (sensor-shift + OIS)

**Lens 3 — Ultra-wide (Bottom):**
- Focal length: 13mm equivalent (0.5x)
- Aperture: f/2.2
- Sensor: 1/2.55"
- Lens diameter: 9.8mm
- Field of view: 120 degrees
- Lens construction: 7 elements including 2 aspherical

**All Lens Details:**
- Lens cover material: Synthetic sapphire with multi-layer AR coating
- AR coating reflectance: 1.5%
- Lens ring material: Black anodized aluminum
- Micro-lens array: Subtle texture visible at extreme macro (< 50mm working distance)

**Other Module Elements:**
- Flash: True Tone LED dual, 5500K, right of camera module, 6mm gap, recessed 0.3mm below module surface
- LiDAR: 4mm x 4mm window, bottom-right of module
