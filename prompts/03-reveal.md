## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Lower-third. The phone is positioned in the lower third of the frame, with the upper two-thirds as breathing room. This creates scale and monumentality — the phone exists in a vast dark space.

**Grid:** Rule of thirds. The phone's horizontal center aligns with the lower third line. The camera module sits in the upper-left intersection of the rule of thirds grid.

**Angle:** Eye-level, slightly low. The viewer is looking at the phone from slightly below eye level — discovering it, not examining it.

**Leading room:** The phone is presented with significant space above it. This negative space is intentional — it elevates the phone to an object of contemplation.

**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (CinemaScope). The wide frame allows the phone to occupy a significant horizontal area while leaving generous breathing room above.
