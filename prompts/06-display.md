The complete front face of the NOVA Elite, showing the display. The phone has rotated 180 degrees from Shot 05 — the back is no longer visible, the front display now faces the camera. The display is active, showing abstract content (no UI elements, no text, no branding).

|---------|---------------|
| Display Size | 6.9 inches (diagonal, rounded corners) |

| Aspect Ratio | 19.5:9 |
| Resolution | 2868 x 1320 pixels (460 PPI) |
| Display Type | LTPO OLED with micro-lens array |
| Edge Geometry | Micro-curved — 2.5D glass with 0.4mm radius edge roll |
| Border Width | 1.7mm left/right, 1.8mm top, 2.2mm bottom (black masked) |

| Peak Brightness | 2,500 nits |

| Color Temperature | Native 6500K, configurable 4000K-9000K |
| Anti-Reflective Coating | 1.5% average reflectance |
| Surface Variation | < 1 micrometer (perfect optical flatness) |

**Display Behavior:**

**Other Front Face Elements:**

**No front key light. No fill. No bounce from the front.** The display provides all the light needed.

## 6. MATERIALS

**Display Glass (Front Cover):**

- **2.5D glass with 0.4mm radius edge roll:** This is the micro-curved edge geometry. The display is NOT flat (which would look cheap) and NOT quad-curved (which would distort content). It is subtly curved at the edges.
- **Anti-reflective coating:** 1.5% average reflectance — reflections are minimal
- **Optical flatness:** Less than 1 micrometer surface variation — perfectly flat
- **Micro-lens array:** Part of the LTPO OLED panel — maximizes brightness efficiency
- **Oleophobic coating:** Reduces fingerprint visibility

**Display Content (abstract):**

- **Color gamut:** 99.5% DCI-P3 — colors should be accurate and saturated
- **Color temperature:** 6500K native — clean, neutral white
- **Brightness:** 2,500 nits peak — display appears luminous
- **Pixel purity:** 99.5% sRGB coverage, no bloom or bleed
- **Content:** Abstract geometric or gradient — NO UI elements, NO text, NO branding
- **No app icons, no widgets, no notification badges, no status bar**

**Display Border (Black Mask):**

- **Width:** 1.7mm left/right, 1.8mm top, 2.2mm bottom
- **Color:** Black (matches the bezel)
- **Purpose:** Defines the edge of the active display area
- **Under normal viewing:** The 0.1mm black mask border is essentially invisible

**Front Camera Area:**

- Small punch-hole or under-display camera at top center
- The camera should be subtly visible but not dominant

**Earpiece/Speaker Grille:**

- Thin slot at top edge
- Laser-ablated titanium or similar precision cut
- Subtle, not prominent

## 7. COMPOSITION

**Framing:** Strict center. The display is centered in the frame, face-on. The viewer is looking directly at the screen. There is perfect symmetry — the center of the display aligns with the center of the frame.

**Grid:** Strict center. The composition is built on absolute center alignment. The display's vertical center is the frame's vertical center. The display's horizontal center is the frame's horizontal center.

**Angle:** Eye-level front. The camera is positioned directly in front of the display, at eye level. The viewer is looking at the screen as if it were their own phone. The angle is exactly perpendicular to the display surface.

**Subject:** The display is the only subject. The display fills the frame. The black mask border is visible as a thin frame around the active area.

**Aspect ratio:** 2.39:1 (CinemaScope). The 19.5:9 display aspect ratio is wider than 16:9 but narrower than 2.39:1. The display will appear with black letterbox bars on top and bottom (or the display will be shown with breathing room above and below).

**Note on aspect ratio handling:** The display's 19.5:9 aspect ratio means that when shown in 2.39:1 CinemaScope, there will be some black space above and below the display. This is correct and expected. The display should NOT be stretched or distorted to fit.
